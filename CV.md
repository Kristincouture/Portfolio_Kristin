# Kristina G. Zayimtsyan

Fashion Designer | Silk Painter | Ceramic Artist  
Founder, **Kristina G. Zayimtsyan Couture**

---

## Professional Profile

Multidisciplinary designer and visual artist specializing in couture fashion, hand-painted silk artworks, and contemporary ceramic pieces. Founder of **Kristina G. Zayimtsyan Couture**, a creative practice that integrates heritage symbolism with refined craftsmanship. Known for developing distinctive design narratives inspired by Armenian culture, including compositions based on the Armenian alphabet and Tree of Life ornaments.
